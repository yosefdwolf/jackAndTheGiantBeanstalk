picture books starring you

Privacy

The short version: we keep what we need to make your book and fix problems, and nothing is sold.

What we keep

  • Your Google account’s name, email address and profile picture, from signing in.
  • The book we make: its pictures and pages.
  • Your wallet: money you add, what each book cost, and any refunds you ask for. Card details go straight to Stripe; we never see or keep them.
  • A step-by-step log of how each book was made (timings, errors and costs). The log never holds the photo or your name and details.
  • Which pages are visited and which buttons are clicked, so we can see where the site gets confusing. This uses a random ID kept in your browser, not a cookie, and never records what you type, your photo, or anything in your book. If your browser asks sites not to track you (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control), none of this is recorded.

Why

To make your book, keep it for you, keep track of your wallet, and find out what went wrong when a book fails.

Who else sees it

The pictures are drawn by OpenAI’s image service, so the photo and the book’s pictures are sent to OpenAI to be drawn. OpenAI says it doesn’t train its models on data sent through its API. Payments are handled by Stripe, which sees your card and email address when you add money. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else.

How long

This is a class project. Books and photos are kept while the project runs. The record of pages and clicks is deleted after 90 days.
